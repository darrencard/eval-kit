# Quote drafting assistant

Illustrative. Acme is an invented company.

## Landing brief

- **Problem:** Estimators spend most of a morning pricing small jobs, so quotes go out a day late and some jobs are lost.
- **Metric:** Tasks finished without a person
- **Bar:** 75%
- **Better:** higher
- **By:** 2026-12-31
- **Non-goals:** No changes to the price book. No automatic sending of quotes.
- **Tokens per task:** 9,000
- **Price per token:** $0.000003
- **The line:** model call. Pricing arithmetic stays in fixed code; only the job description is read by a model.
- **Decides the next step:** Head of Product, Acme
